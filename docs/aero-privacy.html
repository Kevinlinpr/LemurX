Aero 私隱政策Aero Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日Last updated: 19 October 2026

com.osmangroupx.aero

1. 引言1. Introduction

本政策專門適用於奧斯曼集團有限公司（Osman Group Limited，下稱「我們」）及其關聯營運主體發布的 Android 應用 Aero（Personal Agent Browser，包名 com.osmangroupx.aero）。即使你以遊客身份使用、或不向 Agent 提供個人資料，我們仍在本頁公開說明資料如何被收集、使用與分享。我們遵守香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）。Aero 不以兒童為目標用戶。

This policy is specific to Aero (Personal Agent Browser, package com.osmangroupx.aero), the Android app published by Osman Group Limited ("we", "us") and its affiliated operating entities. It discloses how Aero collects, uses and shares user and device data — including when you use Aero as a guest. We comply with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong. Aero is not directed at children.

2. Aero 處理的資料2. Data Aero Handles

  • Google 帳號（若你登入）。Aero 使用 Google 登入。我們會收到 ID 令牌以及你的 Google 顯示名稱、電郵地址與頭像，用以建立或綁定 Aero 帳號。
  • Google account (if you sign in). Aero uses Google Sign-In. We receive an ID token and your Google display name, email address and profile photo so we can create or attach your Aero account.
  • 遊客身份（未登入）。應用在本機保存一把隨機遊客密鑰。伺服器只保存該密鑰的雜湊，保證每台安裝有獨立工作區，遊客之間不串資料。
  • Guest identity (if you do not sign in). The app stores a random guest key on the device. The server keeps only a hash of that key so each install has its own workspace.
  • Agent 對話。你發送的訊息與 Agent 的回覆存在 Aero 雲端，按瀏覽器分頁分開。
  • Agent conversations. Messages you send and replies from the Agent are stored on Aero’s cloud service, one conversation per browser tab.
  • Agent 正在操作的頁面。當你詢問當前頁或讓 Agent 操作時，Aero 可能上傳該分頁的網址、可見區域截圖，以及從無障礙樹取出的文字。頁面內容按不可信資料處理，不當指令執行。
  • Pages the Agent works on. When you ask about a page or to operate it, Aero may send that tab’s URL, a screenshot of the visible page, and text taken from the accessibility tree. Page content is treated as untrusted data, not as instructions.
  • 用於建議的使用記錄。你發給 Agent 的文字、打開或分享的網址、讓它搜尋的查詢，可能用來生成開場建議。我們不會把網頁原文當成可信的用戶畫像來源。
  • Usage that shapes suggestions. Text you type to the Agent, URLs you open or share, and search queries you ask it to run may be used to build suggestions (for example starter chips). We do not treat raw page HTML as a trusted profile source.
  • 裝置橋。Aero 打開時手機會與我們的伺服器保持連線，以便 Agent 操作當前分頁。連線會帶上裝置令牌、遊客或帳號身份，以及應用是否在前台。
  • Device bridge. While Aero is open, the phone keeps a connection to our servers so the Agent can act on the current tab. That connection carries a device token, your guest or account identity, and whether the app is in the foreground.
  • 本機瀏覽器資料。與其他 Chromium 內核瀏覽器一樣，Cookie、歷史、已存密碼與自動填充可以留在本機。除非你在對話裡讓 Agent 使用它們，否則不會上傳到 Agent 伺服器。
  • On-device browser data. Like other Chromium-based browsers, cookies, history, saved passwords and autofill can stay on the device. Those are not uploaded to Aero’s Agent servers unless you ask the Agent to use them.

3. 我們如何使用3. How We Use It

  • 運行你的帳號或遊客工作區，並保證每個分頁的 Agent 互不串台。
  • To run your account or guest workspace and keep each tab’s Agent separate.
  • 生成 Agent 回覆，並完成你要求的瀏覽操作。
  • To generate Agent replies and carry out browsing actions you requested.
  • 顯示個人化開場建議，並保留該分頁的對話。
  • To show personalized starters and remember the conversation on that tab.
  • 僅在你使用 Aero 時維持裝置連線。
  • To keep the device connection alive only while you use Aero.
  • 保障服務安全、防止濫用（例如無效令牌）。
  • To secure the service and prevent abuse (for example invalid tokens).

我們不出售你的個人資料。

We do not sell your personal information.

4. 我們會分享給誰4. Who We Share It With

  • Google。登入由 Google 提供，該步驟適用 Google 私隱權政策。
  • Google. Sign-In is provided by Google. Google’s own policy applies to that step: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • 由我們營運的帳號與 Agent 伺服器：登入、存放對話、向手機下發操作。
  • Our account and Agent servers operated by Osman Group, used to sign you in, store conversations, and send Agent commands to your phone.
  • 我們簽約的語言模型服務：為生成回覆，可能發送對話內容及上文所述的頁面上下文，且僅為此目的。
  • Language-model providers we contract to generate Agent replies. Conversation text and the page context described above may be sent solely to fulfill your request.
  • 網絡搜尋服務：當你或 Agent 代你搜尋時，查詢內容會發給搜尋服務以完成這次搜尋。
  • Web-search providers when you (or the Agent on your behalf) run a web search. The query is sent to complete that search.
  • Google Play。分發、更新以及 Play 的常規安裝診斷。
  • Google Play. Distribution, updates, and Play’s standard app-install diagnostics.

我們不與廣告商分享你的資料，也不用於第三方廣告。

We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. 我們不會做的事5. What We Do Not Do

  • 不向網站注入腳本，不掛調試器來採集頁面。
  • We do not inject scripts into websites or attach a debugger to collect page data.
  • 不讀取你手機上的其他應用。
  • We do not read other apps on your phone.
  • Agent 只操作你看得見的分頁，而且只在 Aero 位於前台時操作。切到後台會掛起這一輪；回到應用後由你點繼續，才在同一個分頁接著做。頁面不會交給另一台你看不見的瀏覽器。
  • The Agent operates only the tab you can see, and only while Aero is in the foreground. Leaving the app suspends that turn. You resume it from the same tab. The page is not handed to another browser you cannot see.

6. 保存期限與你的選擇6. Retention and Your Choices

  • 可以不登入 Google，以遊客身份使用。
  • You can use Aero as a guest without a Google account.
  • 可以隨時「接管」頁面，停止 Agent 對該頁的操作。
  • You can take control of a page at any time; that stops the Agent from driving that page.
  • 退出登入會清除本機的 Google 會話。卸載或清除應用資料會刪除本機瀏覽器資料與遊客密鑰。
  • Signing out removes the Google session from the device. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local browser data and the guest key on that device.
  • 已存在伺服器上的對話會保留，直到你要求刪除。請電郵 mail@osmangroupx.com，主旨寫「Aero data deletion」，我們會刪除你的 Agent 對話與帳號工作區。
  • Conversations already stored on our servers remain until you ask us to delete them. Email mail@osmangroupx.com with the subject “Aero data deletion” and we will delete your Agent conversations and account workspace.

根據香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》，你有權查閱及改正我們持有的關於你的個人資料。

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance of Hong Kong, you have the right to access and correct personal data we hold about you.

7. 兒童7. Children

Aero 不以 13 歲以下（或你所在地區的同等年齡）兒童為目標，也不會故意收集兒童的資料。

Aero is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. 政策的變更8. Changes to This Policy

我們可不時更新本政策，更新後的版本於本頁發佈時生效。

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version takes effect upon posting on this page.
